Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

The Idea for my site is similar to that of Gartic and Gartic Phone. It will be a browser game where players are given reference images to recreate to the best of their ability on a canvas.

Core Content:

  • A digital drawing canvas
  • Single file upload for a reference image to recreate
  • Grading/Scoring algorithm

Stretch Content

  • Multiple scoring difficulties
  • Games longer than a single image
  • Custom game creation
  • Leaderboards for curated global reference images
  • Multiplayer (Feasibility extremely low)

Where will you get all of the content for your site (ie. images & text)?

Most content will be made by me or be user generated content. Some text may be obtained or influenced by ai. Images used in the curated image game mode could be obtained from websites that clearly display what licenses a creator is using.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Because playing games with others is a great way to have fun. It also could be used to improve the skills of artists. In addition, the drawings would be judged objectively rather than subjectively.

Why should a site be built for this?

Because, to my knowledge, there are no websites that offer this version of competitive drawing. I have seached for this kind of website before to play with my friends but was unable to find any. I thought is was good idea that eliminates complaints about me being the judge of their drawings.

What would you like users to do at your site?

I want them to draw and hone their craft. I also want them to have fun, whether it be laughing at the poor drawings of your friends or because of the competive aspect.

What is the goal of the site?

To provide entertainment for outside parties, my friends, and myself. Also to fulfill assignments for this class while making something I will use.
